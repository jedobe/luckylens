## English (en-US)

**App name**
Four-Leaf Clover Finder

**Short description** (≤80 chars)
Snap a clover patch and see where a four-leaf clover may be. No ads, on-device.

**Full description**
Spent ages staring at a clover patch and still can't spot a four-leaf clover? Let your camera help.

Four-Leaf Clover Finder analyses a photo of a clover patch and marks the spots where a four-leaf clover is likely to be. Check those spots first and you'll find one much faster.

■ How it works
1. Take a photo of a clover patch, or pick one from your gallery.
2. The app marks the likely spots.
3. Take a closer look at the marked spots.

■ Why you'll like it
· No ads — buy once, use it ad-free.
· On-device analysis — photos are never uploaded or stored.
· No personal data collected — no sign-up, no login.

■ Good to know
· Marks show likely spots, not guaranteed four-leaf clovers — always check for yourself.
· Works best in good light, close enough that individual leaves are clearly visible.
